Calendar Alarms

Real iPhone alarms for your Google Calendar events. They ring like the Clock app, even in silent mode or Focus.

Alarms from your calendar

Mark an event with ! and it rings before it starts. 30!Exam rings 30 minutes before; 30,15,10!Exam rings three times.

Ring by color

Name your Google Calendar event colors, like Class or Research, and choose which colors ring.

Focus when it rings

Tap Focus on a ringing alarm to start a focus timer for that event.

Where your week went

Weekly stats per label, with what you did and what you skipped.

How it uses Google Calendar

When you sign in with Google, Calendar Alarms reads your calendars and events (read-only) to schedule alarms and show your stats on your iPhone. It never changes your calendar, and your data stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy for details.
