Privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Calendar Alarms ("the app") is an iPhone app that rings alarms before your calendar events. This policy explains what data the app uses and what it does with it. In short: your data stays on your iPhone.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (only if you sign in with Google). With your permission, the app uses the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly to read your calendar list, your events (title, notes, time, color, repeat information and your response) and Google's color list.
  • Calendars on your iPhone. With your permission, the app reads events from the calendars in the iOS Calendar app.
  • Alarms and notifications. The app schedules alarms with iOS and may send you a notification if its access is turned off.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to provide the app's features on your device: deciding which events ring and when, showing your schedule, logging focus time, and showing weekly statistics. The app does not change, create or delete anything in your calendars.

Storage and sharing

  • Event data is processed on your iPhone. Your settings, labels and logs are stored on your iPhone only.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and is sent only to Google to read your calendar.
  • The app has no server. The developer does not receive, store, sell or share your data, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are included.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar Alarms' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Sign out of Google in the app's Settings; this deletes the token from your iPhone and revokes it with Google.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
